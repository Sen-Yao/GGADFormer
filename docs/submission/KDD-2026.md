## 标题

> Leveraging Vectorized Discrepancy for Label-Efficient Graph Anomaly Detection

## 摘要

> Graph anomaly detection (GAD) aims to identify anomalous nodes in graphs, with broad applications in areas such as fraud detection. Semi-supervised GAD, which leverages only a few labeled normal nodes, has attracted growing attention due to the prohibitive cost of obtaining sufficient anomaly labels. Previous studies focus on developing various strategies for learning normal patterns based on labeled nodes. However, empirical results show that existing methods fail to achieve competitive performance with gradually decreasing size of labeled samples. This phenomenon suggests that current approaches cannot effectively capture the underlying patterns of normality under label scarcity, thereby limiting their practical applicability. To address this limitation, we propose VecGAD, a Transformer-based autoencoder framework that effectively leverages supervision from scarce labeled normal nodes through a reconstruction-based mechanism. Specifically, VecGAD computes a directional reconstruction discrepancy vector that precisely captures how each node deviates from normality. Guided by this signal, VecGAD generates pseudo-anomalies that approximate potential deviation patterns, and further constrains them to a hyperspherical shell region to serve as informative hard negatives rather than trivial outliers, which effectively enriches the supervisory signal despite the label scarcity. Extensive experiments demonstrate that VecGAD significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods under various label-scarce settings.
